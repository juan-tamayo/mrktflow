# Module: marketing

Objective: native campaign tools that recover interested shoppers, surface useful offers and show authentic product activity. Depends on platform, identity, catalog, pricing and checkout-orders. Inherits [marketing rules](docs/marketing-rules.md), [engineering conventions](docs/architecture.md), [commands](docs/coding-handoff.md) and [quality requirements](docs/quality-and-operations.md).

## Requirements

- **MKT-01:** Merchant controls campaigns, recovery flows, audience filters, schedule, message templates, offer references and send budgets. Permissions separate draft/edit, activate/send, review moderation and customer export. Campaign activation is a deliberate action; defaults do not begin sending after installation.
- **MKT-02:** Native email signup and preference center store verified contact, purpose-specific consent evidence and suppression state. Recovery, newsletter and stock/price alerts are separate purposes. Opt-out, complaint and hard bounce suppress eligible future sends immediately; checkout email capture alone is not an opt-in.
- **MKT-03:** Detect abandoned carts/checkouts after configurable inactivity; start only for known verified contacts eligible for recovery. Default sequence: 1 hour, 24 hours and 72 hours after the last meaningful shopper change, with a maximum of three messages per abandonment episode. Stop on purchase, resumed activity, suppression, expiry or invalid cart; exclude unknown/in-flight payment outcomes.
- **MKT-04:** Recovery links restore current eligible items to a shopper-controlled cart without exposing addresses, account access or old payment sessions. Reprice/revalidate stock, offer and shipping at checkout; report unavailable products. Links are bounded, revocable and safe against email-scanner GET requests consuming them.
- **MKT-05:** Recovery incentives use unique recipient-bound coupon grants from pricing, optional by step, with spend/eligibility/expiry and budget limits. Retry or repeat click does not create a fresh discount, reset its expiry or duplicate a send intent.
- **MKT-06:** Urgency banners/toasts support actual low stock, a real campaign end time and an eligible discount expiry. Stock is fresh and variant-specific; display neither fabricated stock nor restarting countdowns. Hide stale/unverifiable signals and never claim a basket hold before the commerce engine creates one.
- **MKT-07:** Social proof supports aggregate paid-purchase activity over an explicit time window and moderated verified-purchase reviews. Counts exclude test/unpaid/cancelled/fully refunded orders and fully refunded product-line contributions and meet a minimum aggregation threshold. No random shopper identities, invented sales/viewer counters or customer addresses in public payloads.
- **MKT-08:** Purchasers may leave one editable rating/text review per purchased product after verification. Moderation records reason/history, handles abuse without selectively removing legitimate low ratings, and never edits the customer's sentiment. Ratings include only published real reviews; refunded/revoked purchase eligibility follows the documented policy.
- **MKT-09:** Theme-integrated banners, signup blocks, sale badges and dismissal-capable toasts have targeting, exclusions, frequency limits and accessible rendering. Suppress interruption overlays on checkout, payment, account and download screens. Default: one toast at a time, at least 30 seconds apart and at most three per session.
- **MKT-10:** Native one-off email campaigns use opt-in audiences, test/preview, scheduled batches, pause/resume and suppression checks at dispatch. First-party funnel/UTM/campaign/recovery reporting shows sends, qualified clicks, paid conversions, discounts and refunds; attribution does not claim causation or double-count totals.

## Verification

Tests cover payment/recovery races, resumed carts, purpose opt-outs, duplicate jobs, stale countdown/stock, review ownership/moderation, private data leakage, rate limits and attribution boundaries. Target `php artisan test --filter=Marketing`; browser recovery, toasts, reviews and preference-center journeys. Native tools use the store's configured email transport; SMS, push, ad-buying and a visual general-purpose automation builder are outside v1.
